Livetree Entertainment Studios LLP ("we", "us", "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit www.biggbounce.com or book tickets for BIGG BOUNCE.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information necessary to process your ticket bookings and ensure safety at the event, including:

  • Full Name and Contact Details (Mobile Number, Email Address);
  • Billing information and transaction details (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways like Razorpay);
  • Participant age/category details for ticket verification and safety compliance;
  • Customer support communications, feedback, and inquiry logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Issuing tickets, processing payments, and verifying venue entry;
  • Sending SMS/Email/WhatsApp booking confirmations and event updates;
  • Providing customer service and resolving billing or ticketing issues;
  • Ensuring venue safety and legal compliance under applicable Indian laws.

3. Data Security & Third-Party Gateways

Your payment details are never stored on our servers. All transactions are securely handled by encrypted PCI-DSS compliant payment processors (e.g. Razorpay). We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared only with trusted service partners (such as ticketing systems, payment gateways, or SMS providers) strictly as required to fulfill your booking.
