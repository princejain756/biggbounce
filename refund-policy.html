This Refund Policy applies to all tickets purchased for BIGG BOUNCE — India’s Largest Bouncing Theme Park through our official website (www.biggbounce.com), authorized ticketing partners, box office, or approved sales channels operated by Livetree Entertainment Studios LLP.

1. All Ticket Sales Are Final

Once a ticket booking has been successfully completed, it cannot be cancelled, returned, exchanged, or refunded.

Refunds will not be issued for:

  • Change of plans or personal circumstances;
  • Failure to attend the event or late arrival;
  • Incorrect event date, time slot, or ticket category selected by the customer;
  • Illness, travel disruption, or transportation delays;
  • Failure to bring a valid ticket, booking confirmation, or identification;
  • Refusal of entry due to violation of event safety rules or eligibility restrictions;
  • Removal from the venue because of unsafe or inappropriate behavior;
  • Weather conditions, crowds, queues, or temporary attraction maintenance.

2. No Returns or Exchanges

Tickets are issued for a specific date and 1-hour session time slot. They cannot be exchanged for another date, time slot, category, or event. Tickets have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for cash.

3. Event Cancellation by Organiser

If BIGG BOUNCE cancels the entire event and does not provide an alternative date or session, eligible customers will receive a refund of the ticket’s face value. Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 7–14 business days, subject to bank processing timelines.

4. Rescheduling or Relocation

If the event is rescheduled or relocated within the same city, the original ticket will remain valid for the revised date or session slot.

5. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same booking, or that payment was deducted without a ticket being issued, please contact us within 7 days of the transaction. Confirmed duplicate charges will be reversed/refunded to the original payment method within 7–14 business days.

6. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, limits, or overrides any mandatory rights or remedies available to a customer under applicable Indian consumer protection laws.
