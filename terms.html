Customers must carefully select the correct session date and time slot prior to completing payment. 5. Arrival & Check-In

Ticket holders must arrive at the venue at least 30 minutes prior to their booked session start time for security screening, ticket verification, grip sock fitting, and mandatory safety briefings.

6. Refund Policy

All ticket sales are final. Tickets cannot be cancelled, returned, exchanged, or refunded except in the event of complete cancellation of the Event by the Organiser without a rescheduled date, or as expressly provided in our separate Refund Policy.

7. Participant Eligibility & Safety Rules

  • Age Requirement: Children under 3 years of age are not permitted inside the Bouncing Park. Participants below 18 years require consent from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Health Restrictions: Pregnant women, persons with heart conditions, severe back/neck injuries, or recent surgeries are not permitted inside the bouncing area for safety reasons.
  • Grip Socks: Approved grip socks must be worn inside the bouncing area at all times.
  • Code of Conduct: Dangerous stunts (somersaults, flips), fighting, or rough play are strictly prohibited. The Organiser reserves the right to remove any individual violating safety rules without refund.
